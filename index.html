Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026

Darkfold does not collect any data about you.

There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app makes no network connections. Everything it does happens on your iPhone.

What the app uses on your device

  • Camera. Darkfold uses the camera to take the photos you shoot. The photos are developed and stored on your device.
  • Photo library, adding only. If you choose Save Image when sharing a photo, iOS asks for permission to add it to your library. Darkfold cannot read the photos already in your library.

Where your photos are kept

Photos and contact sheets are kept inside the app on your device. If you have iPhone backups turned on, they are part of that backup, which is handled by Apple or by your computer. Deleting the app deletes the photos kept inside it.

Sharing

When you share a photo or a contact sheet, iOS hands it to the app or person you choose. What happens to it after that is up to that service.

Location

Darkfold does not ask for your location and does not add a location to your photos. Photos keep the technical details the camera records, such as the time they were taken.

Children

Darkfold collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
