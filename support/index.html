How developing works

  • On a foldable iPhone. Shoot with the phone closed. Your photos wait as undeveloped frames. Unfold the phone and they develop on a contact sheet. Open it only halfway to peek, and close it again to keep them undeveloped.
  • On other iPhones. After you shoot, press and hold Hold to develop for two seconds. Let go early and the photos stay undeveloped.

Choosing a look

Slide the strip above the shutter, or swipe across the viewfinder, to choose Negative, Darkroom or Instant. The look is applied when the photos develop, so you cannot preview it. The Date switch prints the date on each photo. Darkroom prints carry no date.

Your photos

  • Developed photos are kept on contact sheets in the album. Tap a photo to see it larger, swipe to move between photos, and pinch or double-tap to zoom.
  • To put a photo in your library, open it, tap Share, and choose Save Image. You can also share a whole contact sheet as one image.
  • Photos are kept inside the app. Deleting the app deletes them, so save the ones you want to keep first.

If the camera does not appear

Open Settings, find Darkfold, and turn on Camera.
